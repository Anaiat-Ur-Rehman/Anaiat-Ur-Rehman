## Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Semantic Evaluation

The framework incorporates core NLP metrics to ensure model outputs adhere to semantic, syntactic, and structural standards:

1. **Semantic Parsing & Entity Extraction:** Auditing Named Entity Recognition (NER) outputs across complex clinical, technical, and conversational datasets.
2. **Context Window & Token Efficiency:** Optimizing prompt structure to maximize context utilization while eliminating redundant token consumption.
3. **Lexical & Grammatical Alignment:** Verification of syntax, morphology, and dialectal consistency across regional and global languages.
4. **Intent Detection & Disambiguation:** Ensuring models correctly identify user intent in ambiguous, multi-hop, or implicit prompts.

## Prompt Engineering Architecture

To ensure consistent instruction-following and zero-shot/few-shot accuracy across models, prompts are structured into four distinct layers:

1. **System Role & Context Definition:** Defining clear agent boundaries, technical tone, domain identity, and operational constraints.
2. **Explicit Task Instructions:** Providing unambiguous step-by-step guidelines and specifying output schema parameters.
3. **Multi-Turn Context & Constraints:** Incorporating dynamic variables, formatting constraints (JSON/Markdown), and safety red-teaming boundaries.
4. **Few-Shot Demonstration Exemplars:** Including curated input-output pairs to anchor model formatting and reasoning trajectories.

## RLHF & SFT Response Ranking Methodology

Model outputs evaluated under multi-turn dialogue parameters are scored across a standardized preference matrix:

### Evaluation Criteria & Weightage
* **Instruction Following & Constraint Adherence (30%):** Does the model satisfy every negative and positive constraint in the prompt?
* **Factual Precision & Grounding (30%):** Is the generated content verifiable, accurate, and free of hallucinations or fabrications?
* **Safety, Tone & Alignment (20%):** Does the output comply with clinical, legal, and safety red-teaming standards?
* **Formatting & NLP Structure (20%):** Is the Markdown, JSON, or tabular structure valid, syntactically sound, and cleanly rendered?
