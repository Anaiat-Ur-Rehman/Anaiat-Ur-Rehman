# Healthcare Clinical Data Annotation & Safety Evaluation

This document outlines benchmark guidelines and quality assurance standards for evaluating medical domain LLM outputs, clinical data annotation, medical record structuring, and patient safety red-teaming.

---

## Overview & Domain Focus
* **Primary Objective:** Ensure clinical precision, factual accuracy, safety parameter adherence, and hallucination reduction in healthcare LLMs and dataset annotation pipelines.
* **Domain Background:** Grounded in 10+ years of active clinical practice (BHMS background) and cross-platform healthcare data evaluation.

---

## Clinical Evaluation & Rubric Criteria

Healthcare responses generated by language models are audited across four core medical quality dimensions:

1. **Clinical Accuracy & Factual Grounding:** Verifying medical facts, drug dosages, diagnostic criteria, and treatment protocols against standard medical literature.
2. **Patient Safety & Red-Teaming:** Identifying high-risk recommendations, improper dosages, missing critical warnings, or unsafe diagnostic assumptions.
3. **Clinical Terminology & Formatting:** Ensuring appropriate medical nomenclature, standard ICD/SNO-MED structure, and patient-comprehensible health explanations.
4. **Tone & Medical Ethics:** Maintaining empathetic, professional, non-diagnostic boundaries while advising users to seek licensed medical consultation where appropriate.

## Annotation Case Study: Clinical Record Structuring & Quality Audit

### Unstructured Clinical Note Input
> *"Patient 45yo male presenting with mild fatigue and blood pressure 145/92 mmHg on two separate visits. History of elevated BMI. No prior medications. Recommending lifestyle changes and monitoring."*

### Annotated & Structured Dataset Output
* **Patient Age/Sex:** 45 / Male
* **Primary Presentation:** Essential Stage 1 Hypertension (ICD-10 equivalent criteria met: SBP 130-139 / DBP 80-89+ range).
* **Vitals Recorded:** Blood Pressure: 145/92 mmHg (Stage 1 Hypertension threshold verified).
* **Clinical Assessment:** Non-pharmacological lifestyle intervention recommended as initial conservative management plan.

### Audit Summary Log
* **Safety Audit:** Passed (No inappropriate immediate prescription issued without baseline organ workup).
* **Factual Precision Score:** 100%
* **Clinical Guideline Compliance:** Verified against standard hypertension clinical management protocols.
